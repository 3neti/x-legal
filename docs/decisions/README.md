# Legal Decision Records

Legal Decision Records capture important legal-architecture choices in a durable
format.

They are similar to engineering ADRs, but their purpose is not only to record a
technical decision. They record why the ecosystem chose a vocabulary, role model,
traceability posture, or regulatory interpretation path at a particular moment.

## Status values

- Proposed: suggested direction under review.
- Accepted: current working position for the ecosystem.
- Superseded: replaced by a later decision.
- Rejected: considered and declined.

## Writing rule

Each decision should preserve uncertainty honestly. A Legal Decision Record may
choose a direction without pretending that external law, counsel review, or
regulatory characterization is settled.
