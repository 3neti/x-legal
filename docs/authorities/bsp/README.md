# BSP Authorities

This folder is reserved for Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas materials that may affect
the legal architecture of x-change.

Examples may eventually include circulars, memoranda, guidance, licensing
materials, FAQs, regulatory issuances, or correspondence relevant to payment
systems, electronic money, operators of payment systems, fund handling, disclosures,
or settlement activity.

Do not add raw legal content without registering it in
`../LEGAL_AUTHORITY_REGISTER.md`.

Do not treat inclusion here as a legal conclusion. Materials in this folder should
remain tied to review status, open questions, and traceability links.
