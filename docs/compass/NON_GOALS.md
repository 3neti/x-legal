# Non-Goals

XLegal exists to make legal architecture visible, disciplined, and traceable. That
mission is strongest when its boundaries are explicit.

This package should not quietly become a substitute for legal judgment, regulatory
engagement, or factual review. It is a constitutional foundation for reasoning, not
an authority that settles the law by itself.

## XLegal does not practice law

XLegal is software documentation and architecture. It does not create an
attorney-client relationship, render advice, or decide what any person or entity is
legally permitted to do.

## XLegal does not replace Philippine banking counsel

External Philippine banking counsel remains necessary for questions involving BSP
classification, registration strategy, customer disclosures, fund handling,
regulatory engagement, and final legal position.

XLegal should make counsel review easier. It should not replace it.

## XLegal does not issue legal opinions

Documents in this package may describe hypotheses, risks, arguments, authorities,
and architectural consequences. They should not be written as final opinions.

When a conclusion requires legal authority, factual investigation, or counsel
review, the document should say so.

## XLegal does not determine final BSP classification

The package does not determine whether x-change is or is not an EMI, OPS, payment
service provider, custody arrangement, escrow arrangement, technology provider, or
any other regulated or unregulated category.

It can preserve candidate characterizations and the architecture required by each
theory. It cannot make the final classification by naming preference.

## XLegal does not guarantee compliance

Traceability improves discipline. It does not guarantee legal compliance.

Compliance depends on actual facts, contracts, disclosures, banking operations,
technical behavior, governance, monitoring, jurisdictional interpretation, and
regulatory posture.

## XLegal does not substitute for regulatory engagement

Where engagement with regulators is required or strategically appropriate, XLegal
can help prepare the architecture and evidence base. It does not replace the
engagement itself.

## XLegal does not turn labels into legal substance

Calling something a mandate, treasury position, settlement instruction, credential,
or journal does not make the legal characterization true. The architecture must be
supported by behavior, evidence, disclosures, and review.

Labels are useful only when they discipline the facts.

## XLegal does not permit architecture to contradict legal intent

Engineering convenience cannot override legal architecture. If future code,
product copy, workflows, or data models contradict the legal intent preserved here,
the contradiction must be surfaced and resolved.

XLegal should make that tension visible early, while it can still shape design.
